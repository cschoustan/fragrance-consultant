# Fragrance Consultant

**A thoughtful, research-led fragrance consultation skill for AI assistants.**

Fragrance Consultant helps people explore perfumes through their own preferences, verified fragrance and perfumer research, balanced perspectives, and practical sampling advice. It is designed to be portable across AI assistants rather than tied to one platform.

## What it does

- **Personalized discovery** — A single, five-question intake covering desired impression, scent direction, projection, occasions, and budget.
- **Curated recommendations** — A focused shortlist with clear reasons, meaningful trade-offs, and sampling priorities.
- **Perfumer research** — Verified creator credits, documented creative perspectives, and other attributable works.
- **Evidence-aware analysis** — Separates official product details, sourced personal experiences, and the consultant's interpretation.
- **Practical sampling guidance** — Helps compare perfumes on paper and skin, from opening to drydown.

## How to use

1. Open [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md).
2. In an AI assistant that supports skills, add the file using that assistant's supported skill-installation workflow.
3. In other AI assistants, provide the contents of `SKILL.md` as instructions or relevant context.
4. Ask something like: **“Help me find a signature fragrance.”** The assistant can use the five-question questionnaire when your preferences are not yet clear.

The core workflow works in plain text. Web research, product images, and interactive questionnaires can enrich the experience when the assistant supports them. Research quality and automatic skill activation depend on the platform and its available tools.

## Consultation flow

**Discover preferences → Research candidates → Compare fragrances and perfumers → Recommend sampling priorities → Refine from feedback**

The skill does not prescribe favorite brands or a fixed ranking. Recommendations are derived from the current user's needs and available evidence.
